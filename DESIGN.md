# Design System: Budget

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "One Shell, Eight Wardrobes"**

Budget has one body and eight sets of clothes. The shell (layout, navigation, controls, the review inbox, the due list, every semantic rule) is identical in every theme. A theme lends only four things: its type, its palette, its density, and one signature move. Switching from Sakura to Courtside should feel like the same app dressed for a different person, never like a different app. That is how "personal" (PRODUCT.md principle 5) stays compatible with "review, don't type": the muscle memory never moves.

The surface is a phone-first working inbox, not a dashboard. Home leads with the stack of captured transactions waiting for a tap; only the front card is lifted, everything else sits flat on hairlines. Money is set large in the theme's display or amount face with tabular numerals, and signs, words and icons carry meaning before colour does. Density is comfortable and thumb-led: 52px controls, 16px gutters, 28px between sections.

Themes are tokens on `[data-theme="<id>"]` with a `[data-mode="dark"]` overlay; the frontmatter records every resolved value per theme and mode as `<theme>-<mode>-<role>`, and `.impeccable/design.json` (`extensions.themeVariables`) carries the same values as raw declaration blocks ready to paste into `globals.css`. Themes are never labelled by gender.

**Key Characteristics:**
- One shared shell; a theme changes type, palette, density and one signature move, nothing structural.
- Review inbox first: one lifted card, one decision, Confirm in thumb reach.
- Signed, worded money: "−" for expenses in ink, "+" for income in the income colour, plus a "Money in / Money out" label with an arrow icon.
- Every theme ships light and dark; text role pairs (ink, muted, accent, income, danger on their surfaces, on-accent on accent, tile ink on tile) clear WCAG AA 4.5:1 in all sixteen.
- Container-query layout: one tree that becomes a three-column desktop at 760px of app width.

## Colors

Every theme defines the same 23 role variables (plus theme-only signature variables), so components never name a hex; they name a role.

### Primary
- **Accent** (`--accent`): Confirm and Add, the active tab, the review count, links, focus rings, selection. One accent per theme: Sakura's deep cherry pink, Strawberry's strawberry red, Bubblegum's magenta, Barako's roasted brown, Courtside's basketball orange, Denim's indigo (amber thread in dark), Monochrome's pure black or white, Coal & Brass's aged brass.
- **Text on Accent** (`--on-accent`): text and icons on accent fills.
- **Accent Wash** (`--accent-soft`): the current rail item and the avatar disc; Strawberry Milk also puts chips on it.

### Semantic
- **Income** (`--income`): income amounts, "Money in" labels, income in the peek line and the desktop queue. Always paired with "+" and the arrow-down-left icon.
- **Danger** (`--danger`): overdue row border, overdue tile fill, overdue label and its alert icon, duplicate-flag icon.
- **Danger Wash** (`--danger-soft`): background of the "Possible duplicate." flag, with ink text.

### Data
- **Series 1 to 5** (`--s1` to `--s5`): spending-bar segments and legend dots, in category order. Each theme draws its series from its own palette (Monochrome uses five greys). Never used for meaning outside charts, except Bubblegum's sticker fills (`--s4`, `--s5`) and Sakura's petals (`--s2`).

### Neutral
- **Page Background** (`--bg`): the app canvas; also toast text.
- **Card Surface** (`--surface`): cards, due rows, account lists, rail, tab bar.
- **Recessed Surface** (`--surface-2`): institution badges, chips, the spending-bar track, rail hover, the Ignore drag hint.
- **Primary Text** (`--ink`): all body text and expense amounts; toast fill.
- **Secondary Text** (`--muted`): sources, sublabels, legend values, inactive tabs and rail items.
- **Hairline** (`--line`) and **Strong Line** (`--line-strong`): card borders and row dividers; strong line on secondary buttons, kbd caps, dashed empty-state border, Strawberry's carton edge.

### Theme signature colours
- **Due Tile Fill / Ink** (`--tile-bg`, `--tile-ink`): the date tile in Due soon. Absent in Coal & Brass, which outlines the tile in accent instead; Barako's is transparent (a stamp).
- **Stitch Thread** (`--stitch`, Denim only): the dashed seam on the front card and month card.
- **Brass** (`--brass-2` plate, `--brass-3` edge, `--brass-ink` lettering; Coal & Brass only). `--brass-1` is defined but unused.
- Fixed signature colours hard-coded in the build (scoreboard frame, patch stitch, sticker edge, overdue tile ink) are recorded as named tokens in the frontmatter so the port can lift them into variables.

### Named Rules
**The Never Colour Alone Rule.** Direction of money is carried by sign, word and icon first. Expenses: ink colour, "−" (U+2212, not a hyphen), "Money out" with arrow-up-right. Income: `--income` colour, "+", "Money in" with arrow-down-left. A colour-blind user reading any theme in greyscale must lose nothing.

**The Debt Is Not Red Rule.** Owed balances on credit, BNPL and loan accounts are shown as the absolute amount in ink with a small muted "owed" label. Danger is reserved for things that need action now (overdue, possible duplicate), never for "this is a debt".

**The Role, Not Hex Rule.** Components reference role variables only. A new theme is a new value set for the same 23 roles; a new component never introduces a theme-specific colour outside the signature block.

## Typography

**Display Font:** per theme (`--font-display`): Zen Maru Gothic, Fredoka, Bagel Fat One, Young Serif, Big Shoulders Display, Archivo, Geist, Marcellus.
**Body Font:** per theme (`--font-body`): Zen Maru Gothic, Fredoka, Rubik, Work Sans, Barlow, Archivo, Geist, Manrope.
**Amount Font:** `--font-amount`, which is the display face in every theme except Coal & Brass (Manrope 700, so figures stay crisp under Marcellus headings).

**Character:** each theme picks a pairing that matches its world (rounded Japanese gothic for Sakura, chubby rounded sans for Strawberry, fat bubble display for Bubblegum, warm slab-serif for Barako, condensed stadium signage for Courtside, wide workwear grotesque for Denim, tight neo-grotesque for Monochrome, inscriptional capitals for Coal & Brass), while the size ramp is shared.

### Hierarchy
- **Amount Hero** (theme amount weight, 40px phone / 48px desktop, line-height 1, tabular): the front review card amount. Cents drop to 0.5em; sign sits 4px before the peso. Courtside 54px; Monochrome 52px at -0.04em.
- **Headline, Month** (display weight, 26px, 1.1): the month switcher in the top bar. Courtside 34px; Monochrome 32px; Denim at 115% width.
- **Amount Summary** (26px, tabular): month spent figure.
- **Due Day** (24px, line-height 1, tabular): the date on the due tile. Courtside 28px with +0.04em.
- **Title, Section** (display weight, 19px, 1.2): "To review", "Due soon", "Accounts". Courtside 30px at line-height 1; Denim 115% width; Coal & Brass +0.01em.
- **Title, Merchant** (600, 17px): merchant on the review card.
- **Button** (600, 16px).
- **Body** (400, 15px, 1.45): default; empty-state copy capped at 40ch.
- **Row Title** (600, 14 to 14.5px) and **Row Amount** (700, tabular).
- **Label** (500 to 600, 13px): chips, sources, flags, drag hints, overdue label.
- **Caption** (12 to 12.5px): account sublabels, estimates, due "when" labels.
- **Tab Label** (500, 11.5px).

### Named Rules
**The Tabular Money Rule.** Every amount, count and date digit uses `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`. Pesos are written ₱ with en-PH grouping and always two decimals; only the spending legend drops a trailing ".00".

**The Sentence Case Rule.** Headings, labels and group names are sentence case at their natural size. No uppercase eyebrows or kickers above headings; group labels ("Cash & e-wallets") are 13px muted 600, nothing more.

## Layout

The app is a single tree inside a size container named `app`; layout responds to the app's width, not the viewport.

**Phone (below 760px of app width).** One column. Main padding is 0 16px 24px with 28px between sections, in order: top bar (month switcher left, 38px avatar right), To review (title, count pill, subline, card stack, Ignore and Confirm), Due soon, month spending, Accounts. The card stack shows the front card plus two cards behind it (offset 34px at 96% and 52px at 92% / 55% opacity) with a "Next:" peek line on the second. Ignore and Confirm sit in a 1 : 1.4 grid so Confirm is the larger target on the thumb side. The bottom tab bar is sticky with five equal slots: Home, Review (count badge), a centre Add (52px accent square in the control radius), Accounts, More; it honours `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`. The undo toast floats 84px above the bottom, clear of the tab bar. The spending legend becomes two columns from 460px.

**Desktop (760px and up).** A three-column grid: a 220px sticky left rail, a fluid main column, and a 400px right column. The rail holds the brand, a 44px "Add transaction" primary button, then Home, Review (badge), Accounts, Cards & loans, Reports, with Settings pinned to the foot. Main (padding 8px 32px 40px) holds the review inbox as a 1.2fr / 1fr grid (front card and actions left, an "Up next" queue list right, gap 24px), keyboard hints (C confirm, I ignore, U undo), and the month spending card. The right column (padding 22px 24px 40px, hairline left border, background mixed 60% page and 40% surface) holds Due soon and Accounts. The tab bar is hidden; the toast is centred at the bottom, at least 340px wide.

The same section components render in both places; the container query only decides which copy is visible, so no section has a phone-only or desktop-only variant.

### Named Rules
**The Thumb Rule.** On phone, Confirm, Add and the active decision are always in the bottom half; nothing that confirms or adds money lives in the top bar.

**The Due Before Spent Rule.** Due soon always comes before spending: directly after the inbox on phone, at the top of the right column on desktop.

## Elevation & Depth

A hybrid: surfaces are flat and separated by hairlines and tonal steps (`--bg` canvas, `--surface` cards, `--surface-2` recesses), and exactly one thing carries a theme shadow: the review card, through `--card-shadow`. Due rows, account lists and the month card have a border and no shadow. Each theme tunes `--card-shadow` (values per theme and mode in the sidecar's `shadows`); Monochrome sets it to none and relies on inversion instead. The only other shadows are functional: the centre Add button, the toast, and the theme signatures listed below.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Default card** (`0 1px 2px rgb(0 0 0 / .04), 0 8px 24px -12px rgb(0 0 0 / .18)`): shared default, used by Courtside, Denim and Coal & Brass in light mode.
- **Tinted bloom** (Sakura `0 10px 30px -14px rgb(194 48 106 / .35)`, Bubblegum `0 2px 0 rgb(42 17 64 / .06), 0 18px 32px -16px rgb(139 92 246 / .45)`, Barako `0 1px 0 rgb(42 25 15 / .08), 0 12px 24px -16px rgb(42 25 15 / .5)`): light-mode card shadow tinted with the theme's own hue.
- **Carton ledge** (Strawberry Milk, `0 6px 0 -1px var(--line-strong), 0 14px 24px -14px rgb(201 49 88 / .45)`; dark swaps the second layer to `rgb(0 0 0 / .6)`): a deliberate 0-blur first layer that reads as the 6px bottom lip of a milk carton. Strawberry Milk's signature; kept on purpose.
- **Dark-mode drop** (`0 12px 30px -14px rgb(0 0 0 / .6)` to `0 14px 30px -18px rgb(0 0 0 / .85)`): dark modes swap tinted blooms for plain black.
- **Add button** (`0 6px 14px -6px rgb(0 0 0 / .35)`) and **Toast** (`0 12px 30px -12px rgb(0 0 0 / .5)`).

### Named Rules
**The One Lifted Card Rule.** Only the card you are deciding on is lifted. If a new surface wants a shadow, it must be the thing the user acts on next; otherwise it gets a hairline.

**The Signature Stays Home Rule.** Hard-edged or ornamental depth (Strawberry's carton ledge, Bubblegum's sticker ring, Courtside's scoreboard glow) belongs to its theme only and never becomes a shared style.

## Shapes

Radius is a theme voice expressed through three variables: `--radius-card` (cards, due rows, lists), `--radius-ctl` (buttons, rail items, flags, toast, centre Add) and `--radius-tile` (the due tile). Values per theme are in the frontmatter `rounded` group. The range runs from Monochrome's near-square 4px / 4px / 2px to Sakura's asymmetric petal cut (30px 6px 30px 6px on cards), with Strawberry Milk and Bubblegum making every control a full pill and Barako turning the due tile into a circle (a rubber stamp).

Shared, theme-independent shapes: pills (999px) for chips, counts, badges, the spending bar and segmented toggles; 12px institution badges (10px in account rows, 9px in the desktop queue; Sakura cuts them 14px 4px 14px 4px); 3px legend dots; 50% avatar. Borders are 1px `--line` by default, 2px `--line-strong` in Strawberry Milk; the empty state uses a 1.5px dashed `--line-strong` border.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Shape:** the theme control radius (`--radius-ctl`), 52px minimum height, 0 18px padding, 600 at 16px, icon plus label with 8px gap.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** put every theme value in a CSS custom property on `[data-theme]` / `[data-mode="dark"]` and have components read roles only (`--accent`, `--surface`, `--radius-ctl`, `--card-shadow`).
- **Do** write expenses as ink with "−" (U+2212) and income in `--income` with "+", plus the "Money out / Money in" label and arrow icon, everywhere an amount has a direction (card, peek, queue).
- **Do** mark overdue with all three: danger colour, the circle-alert icon, and words ("1 day late").
- **Do** label every projection with the word "Estimate" and the trending-up icon, in muted caption text.
- **Do** switch layout with `@container app (min-width: 760px)`: rail 220px, main fluid, right column 400px; below it, the bottom tab bar with the centre Add.
- **Do** keep Strawberry Milk's 6px carton ledge (`--card-shadow` with a 0-blur first layer) as that theme's signature.
- **Do** keep Bubblegum's sticker ring and drop shadow; it is intentional.
- **Do** keep Coal & Brass's brass as a flat plate (brass-2 fill, brass-3 1px inset edge, brass-ink text) on due amounts.
- **Do** keep Denim's leather patch flat with exactly one stitch line.
- **Do** honour `prefers-reduced-motion` by collapsing all transitions and the petal drift.
- **Do** label demo data as sample data on any preview surface.

### Don't:
- **Don't** let a theme change layout, navigation order, control size or semantic rules; a theme lends type, palette, density and one signature move only.
- **Don't** convey income, expense or status by colour alone.
- **Don't** colour owed balances with danger; use the "owed" label.
- **Don't** fake metal in Coal & Brass: no gradients, bevels, highlights or sheen on the brass plate (`--brass-1` stays unused).
- **Don't** add more stitch lines, rivets or texture to Denim's patch.
- **Don't** carry a theme's signature (carton ledge, sticker ring, scoreboard glow, receipt edge, petal cut) into another theme or into shared components.
- **Don't** lift any surface other than the review card with `--card-shadow`.
- **Don't** add uppercase eyebrows or kickers above section titles.
- **Don't** label or group themes by gender.
